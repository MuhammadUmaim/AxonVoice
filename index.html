Questions

Probably what you're wondering.

Can I just try it before buying anything?
Yes — that's the point of Emili. Hit any “Talk to Emili” button on this page and she picks up in your browser. Ask her about a roof, a leak or a storm claim and see how she handles it. It's the real agent, not a recording.
Will callers know it's an AI?
Most don't, and the agent is disclosed wherever the law requires it. It uses natural turn-taking, handles interruptions and never reads from a script like a robot. If someone asks for a human, it hands over immediately.
What happens if the agent doesn't know the answer?
It says so honestly, takes a message, and routes the call — either to you, to voicemail with a summary, or to a callback that's scheduled automatically. It never invents prices, warranty terms or availability.
Do I need to change my phone number?
No. We either forward your existing number to the agent, or set up a new one for overflow and after-hours. Your main line keeps working exactly as it does today.
Which systems can it connect to?
Jobber, JobNimbus, ServiceTitan, AccuLynx, Google Calendar, Outlook, GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Twilio and anything with an API. If your CRM has one, I'll wire it up — that's the part I enjoy.
What do I own at the end?
Everything: the scripts, the prompts, the knowledge base, the automations, and the accounts. If you ever leave, you take it all with you and it keeps running.
How long until it's live?
Two weeks is typical for a single agent with calendar booking. Complex integrations can take a little longer, and I'll give you a real date before we start — not a range.
